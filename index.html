About Me

Hi! I am Maoye Huang, a Master's student in Computer Science at Fujian Normal University, advised by Associate Professor Peng Shi. I received my B.S. degree in Computer Science from Minjiang University in 2023, where I worked with Professor Zuoyong Li.

My research centers on medical artificial intelligence, particularly the development of reliable representation and generative learning methods for clinical images and physiological signals. I also visited Zhengzhou University in 2024, working with Associate Research Fellow Haoyi Fan on ECG signal processing and generation.

Research Interests

  • Medical Image Processing: learning diagnostic representations from limited annotations and preserving clinically important structures.
  • Generative and Representation Learning: diffusion models, self-supervised learning, contrastive learning, and few-shot adaptation.
  • Multimodal Medical Signals: fine-grained alignment between physiological waveforms, clinical text, and diagnostic semantics.

PhD Applications - Fall 2027

I am currently applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027. My research interests center on high-quality representation learning and multimodal intelligence in complex and low-resource data settings, with particular interests in multimodal and multi-granularity representation learning, data-efficient learning and generalization, and structure- and semantics-guided generative learning. I am also broadly interested in AI Agents, World Models, Embodied AI, Multimodal Learning, Visual Representation Learning, and Generalization.
