About Me

Hi! I am Maoye Huang, a Master's student in Computer Science at the College of Computer and Cyber Security, Fujian Normal University, advised by Assoc. Prof. Peng Shi. I received my B.S. degree in Computer Science (AI Innovation Class) from Minjiang University in 2023, under the supervision of Prof. Zuoyong Li.

My research focuses on medical image processing, with particular interests in diffusion generative models, self-supervised representation learning, multimodal medical signal processing, and few-shot adaptation. I am also broadly interested in AI Agents, World Models, Embodied AI, and Multimodal Learning.
